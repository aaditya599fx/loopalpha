# LoopAlpha experimental protocol

The hypothesis is that supplying exact occurrence gradients improves optimization of a shared residual block. The null is that it offers no advantage under comparable tuning and compute. Synthetic evidence only addresses the specified generators.

- Primary endpoints are held-out MSE at the last validation checkpoint reached within 120 ms of wall-clock training, first time and step at which validation MSE reaches 0.20, and failure frequency. Each run is capped at 160 steps and validates every 10 steps. Report uncaptured time checkpoints and unreached thresholds as missing. Compare these endpoints on the toy task. Report fixed-step MSE alongside them. Validation and time-checkpoint overhead count toward elapsed training time.
- Use seeds 17, 41 and 73. Give each optimizer three learning rates and choose the rate by the mean final validation MSE across the three seeds. Repeat comparisons on each task and fold. A rate that fails in any seed is ineligible. Divergence counts include tuning trials and final runs as separate denominators.
- Compare momentum SGD, AdamW, eligible hidden-matrix Muon with AdamW for the rest, and the depth-aware AdamW update. Ablate clipping, late-depth weights and separate moments. Compare loop depths 1/2/4/8 and a compute-shape-matched untied block stack. Depth sweeps use the AdamW rate from the depth-4 model. The untied stack uses more parameters.
- Evaluate daily Pearson and Spearman correlations. Quantify directional stability and report a circular block bootstrap interval per seed and fold. The intervals cover date variation under the chosen block length. They do not model seed variation.
- Test two chronological market folds. The first is a historical evaluation and the second is the terminal holdout. Each fold excludes any training or validation decision whose target exits at or after the next partition opens. Fix configuration before evaluation. Include within-date training label permutation and independent Gaussian feature replacement as negative controls.
- Rank on the signal date and trade non-overlapping equal-dollar long/short cohorts from the next open. Charge 0, 5, 10 and 25 bps per dollar traded. Annualize the holding-period Sharpe by sqrt(252 / horizon), using a zero risk-free rate. The ledger records fills and prices for independent auditing.
